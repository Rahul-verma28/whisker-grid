Questions

Is Whisker Grid free?

Yes! The whole game is free to play. It's supported by ads, and most of them are optional videos you choose to watch for a bonus, like a hint or double coins.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. You can play every level offline. An internet connection is only needed to load optional reward videos.

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

Mark cells that can't hold a friend with ✕. Look for colours or rows with only one free cell left. If you're still stuck, use a hint.

Will my progress be saved?

Yes. Your levels, stars, coins and friends are saved on your phone automatically. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Does the game collect my personal data?

We don't ask for any personal information and there's no account. Our ad partner, Google AdMob, may use device identifiers to show ads. See our privacy policy.
