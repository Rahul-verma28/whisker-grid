Privacy Policy

Whisker Grid: Logic Puzzle · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how Whisker Grid ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is published by Rahul Verma.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email address, or any account. There is no login.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, friends and settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The app shows ads using Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information to show and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and settings in a local file on your phone. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, we show ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Many ads are optional videos that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about your interactions with ads, plus app performance and diagnostics data

This information is used to deliver ads, personalise them where permitted, measure them, and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice by reinstalling the app. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration permission for gentle haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's Settings. The ads SDK uses internet access to load ads. The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

Children's privacy

Whisker Grid is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's own privacy policy and retention practices.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. We don't hold personal data about you on our servers. For ad-related data, use the device settings above or contact Google directly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
