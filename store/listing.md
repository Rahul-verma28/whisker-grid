## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
Find the hidden friends in Whisker Grid, a cozy logic puzzle with adorable 3D animals!

Every board is split into colourful regions. Your job: place one friend in every row, every column and every colour, and make sure no two friends ever touch, not even diagonally. Simple to learn, deeply satisfying to master.

🐱 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a square to mark it with an X
• Double-tap to place a friend
• Drag across squares to mark many at once
• Use logic, not luck: every puzzle has exactly one solution

✨ WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• 100 hand-picked levels that grow from gentle 4×4 boards to brain-bending 10×10 grids
• A new Daily Challenge every day
• Earn up to 3 stars on every level: no mistakes and no hints for a perfect score
• Unlock cute 3D friends: Mochi the cat, Biscuit the dog, Clover the bunny and Pip the mouse
• Your friends dance, hop and cheer when you solve a puzzle
• Four beautiful themes, including a calm night mode
• Relaxing music and gentle sound effects (switch them off any time)
• Helpful hints when you're stuck, plus Undo and quick Clear
• No timers to beat and no pressure. Play at your own pace
• Works offline

🧠 GOOD FOR YOUR BRAIN
Whisker Grid trains focus, pattern spotting and deduction. It's perfect for a coffee break, the commute, or winding down before bed.

If you enjoy sudoku, nonograms, star battle or queens-style puzzles, you'll feel right at home.

Download Whisker Grid and start finding friends today! 🐾
```
